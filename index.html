Command List for Siri

Support

A practical guide to Siri voice commands on iPhone and iPad, from everyday requests to the new Siri AI in iOS 27. Also known as Befehlsliste für Siri, Liste de commandes pour Siri and Lista de comandos para Siri.

Need help, or found a command that doesn’t work?

Open an issue on GitHub. Tell us which command you tried, what Siri answered, your device, system version and the language Siri is set to.

Common questions

Why doesn’t Siri understand a command from the app?

Siri’s abilities depend on your device, system version, language and region, and sometimes on apps or accessories you’ve set up. Each command in the app shows a badge when it needs something special, for example Apple Intelligence, ChatGPT, Siri AI or smart home accessories. Small differences in wording are usually fine, so feel free to say it your way.

What is Siri AI, and can I use it?

Siri AI is the new version of Siri introduced with iOS 27. It needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later and a supported language: English, and since iOS 27.2 also French and Spanish, among others. It is not yet available in German, and not yet on iPhone in the EU. Commands marked “Siri AI” show what becomes possible once it is available to you.

How does “How do I…?” work?

Type what you want to do in your own words. The app finds matching commands on your device. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple’s on-device language model also writes a short answer. Your questions are never sent anywhere.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for the app. Choose the small or medium size. On the Lock Screen, touch and hold, tap Customize, and add the widget below the clock.

Can I ask Siri for a tip?

Yes. Say “Hey Siri, give me a Siri tip from Command List” (in German: “Gib mir einen Siri-Tipp von Befehlsliste”). Siri reads today’s tip.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, French and Spanish. The app follows your iPhone’s language. You can change it in Settings › Apps › the app › Language.

Where are my favorites and progress kept?

On your device only. See the privacy policy.
