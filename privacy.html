Command List for Siri

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

In short: Command List for Siri (Befehlsliste für Siri) does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps your favorite commands and the commands you marked as tried. They are stored only on your device, inside the app’s own storage. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, that backup can include the app’s data, under Apple’s terms and your own backup settings.

Questions you type in “How do I…?”

Questions are processed entirely on your device. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple’s on-device language model to write a short answer. Questions are not stored after you leave the screen and are never sent to the developer or any server.

Siri, Shortcuts and the widget

When you ask Siri for a tip from the app, or add the app’s widget, the app only provides the tip of the day, which is built into the app. Siri requests themselves are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and your Siri settings.

What the app does not do

  • It has no sign-in and no account.
  • It has no developer-operated analytics or crash reporting SDK. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s terms.
  • It shows no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It makes no network requests. All commands are inside the app.
  • It asks for no permissions such as microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Sharing a command

When you choose Copy or Share, the phrase is placed on the clipboard or passed to the system share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send or paste it.

Deleting your data

Remove a favorite with its star, or turn off “I tried it”. Deleting the app removes all of its local data.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
